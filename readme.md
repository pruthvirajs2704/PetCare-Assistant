# 🐾 Pet Care Assistant

### A Simple Web-Based Platform for Pet Care Services

Pet Care Assistant is a web-based application designed to help pet owners easily access essential pet care services and information through a simple and user-friendly interface.

The application brings important pet care features together in one place, including **pet grooming, nutrition, service booking, and location assistance**.

---

## 🌟 Project Overview

Taking care of pets involves managing different needs such as grooming, nutrition and regular services.

The **Pet Care Assistant** provides a centralized platform where users can explore pet care options and access useful services through an easy-to-use interface.

The project focuses on creating a clean, simple and interactive experience for pet owners.

---

## ✨ Key Features

### 🐕 Pet Grooming

Provides users with information and options related to pet grooming and care.

Users can explore grooming-related services designed to help maintain their pet's health, hygiene and appearance.

---

### 🥗 Pet Nutrition

Provides useful information related to pet nutrition and healthy food choices.

The nutrition section helps pet owners understand the importance of providing appropriate food and maintaining a healthy diet for their pets.

---

### 📅 Service Booking

Allows users to explore available pet care services and make a booking through the application.

The booking functionality provides a simple way for users to request pet care services.

---

### 📍 Location

Provides location-related assistance to help users find or access pet care services.

The location section is designed to make it easier for users to identify nearby or relevant pet care facilities.
